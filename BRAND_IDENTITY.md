## 1. Brand Character & Personality

NEXU ATELIER is not a drop-shipping site, a mass-market discount bazaar, or an imitation fashion label. It is a curated international marketplace platform bringing together the finest independent textile looms, bespoke acoustic labs, and sculptural homeware ateliers.

### Personality Pillars
- **Curated, Not Cluttered:** We celebrate white space. Every product has room to breathe.
- **Architectural, Not Decorative:** Structure carries meaning. We eschew superfluous gradients, random stickers, and fake urgency tickers.
- **Authoritative, Not Aggressive:** Pricing is transparent, delivery is carbon-neutral, and escrow guarantees protect both patron and maker.
- **Rooted, Yet Global:** We honor ancient Indian traditions (Chanderi silk, Moradabad brass, Jaipur block print) while engineering for discerning global collectors.

## 2. Visual Identity Pillars

### A. The 85/15 Neutral Canvas Law
85% to 90% of every screen is composed of calm neutral tones:
- **Canvas:** `#F6F7F9` (Warm editorial stone)
- **Surfaces:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white museum cardstock)
- **Subtle Layering:** `#F8FAFC` (Secondary structural background)

The remaining 10–15% is intentional:
- **Deep Slate:** `#17191F` for authoritative headlines and primary action triggers.
- **Signature NEXU Blue:** `#2457C5` for focused interactive states, verified links, and key navigation highlights.
- **Artisan Emerald:** `#15803D` for verified maker provenance and stock assurances.

### B. Portrait Ratio Law (`4:5`)
All garment, jewelry, acoustic, and object showcases enforce a portrait ratio of `aspect-[4/5]`. This mirrors high-fashion editorial magazines and ensures that from collar to hem, every garment is displayed in full resolution without awkward automatic cropping.

### C. Truth in Commerce
- **No Fake Countdown Clocks:** Deals do not expire in 07:42:18 unless tied to a real campaign.
- **No Fabricated Badges:** "Studio Allocation of the Day" and "1-Click Auto Upgrade" are forbidden on retail showcases.
- **Truthful Stock:** Badges like "Only 3 Left" reflect actual inventory numbers in PostgreSQL.
