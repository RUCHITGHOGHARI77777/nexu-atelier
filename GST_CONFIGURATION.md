## Quick Reference Summary

1. **Governing Authorities:** Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) & GST Council.
2. **Key Artisan Notification:** **Notification No. 13/2025-Central Tax (Rate), dated 17 September 2025** (Applies to brass handicrafts, stone crafts, and hand-embroidered textiles).
3. **Statutory HSN Categories:**
   - **HSN 5007:** Woven Silk Fabrics $\rightarrow$ **5.0% GST**
   - **HSN 5208:** Woven Cotton Fabrics $\rightarrow$ **5.0% GST**
   - **HSN 6204:** Stitched Garments / Sarees $\rightarrow$ **5.0%** ($\le ₹1,000$) / **12.0%** ($> ₹1,000$)
   - **HSN 7418 / 7419:** Brass Lost-Wax Crafts $\rightarrow$ **12.0% GST** (Handicrafts Schedule)
   - **HSN 6912 / 6913:** Ceramic Art & Pottery $\rightarrow$ **12.0%** (5.0% for clay terracotta)
   - **HSN 6403:** Leather Mojari & Kolhapuri $\rightarrow$ **5.0%** ($\le ₹1,000$) / **12.0%** ($> ₹1,000$)
   - **HSN 8518:** Acoustics & Tech $\rightarrow$ **18.0% GST**
4. **Jurisdiction Split:**
   - Intra-State: 50% CGST + 50% SGST
   - Inter-State: 100% IGST
5. **Integer Paise Compliance:** All values extracted with zero floating point variance.
