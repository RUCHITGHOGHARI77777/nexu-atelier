02 / Market Context Industry Inefficiencies

The Fragmented Commerce Problem

Traditional retail models force artisan and precision studios into impossible compromises between discovery and commercial viability.

Customer Friction

Disjointed Discovery

Patrons seeking verified heritage textiles and precision design must navigate dozens of disconnected independent websites, creating separate accounts, entering payment details repeatedly, and managing tracking across multiple couriers.

Supplier Burden

Commercial & Tax Overhead

Independent studios lack integrated tools to handle statutory Indian GST calculations (intra-state CGST/SGST vs inter-state IGST), courier API dispatch, automated escrow reconciliations, and variant inventory controls.

Mass Market Trap

Erosion of Brand Equity

Generic mega-marketplaces treat bespoke artisan creations as interchangeable SKUs, charging steep 25–35% take rates, hiding maker origins, and subjecting studios to predatory algorithmic price wars.
